# Support

Precedent is free and maintained by one developer, Jayanth Dolai. This page is the support agreement for the app.

## How to get help

Open an issue at **https://github.com/demonjd2026-afk/precedent/issues**. Include what you did, what you expected, and what happened. Don't paste customer data, decision text or account ids into a public issue.

For a security problem, don't open a public issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## Hours and response times

Support hours are Monday to Friday, 10:00 to 18:00 India Standard Time (UTC+5:30), excluding Indian public holidays.

| Severity | Example | First response |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | The app breaks Jira pages, or data is exposed to the wrong people | 1 business day |
| High | A main feature (capture, conflict check, register) doesn't work | 2 business days |
| Normal | Questions, minor bugs, feature requests | 5 business days |

These are targets, not guarantees. There's no paid support tier.

## Availability

Precedent runs entirely on Atlassian's Forge platform, so its availability follows Atlassian Cloud's: https://status.atlassian.com. There's no separate uptime commitment.

## Data

What the app stores, and how it's removed, is described in [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).
